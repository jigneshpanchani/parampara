Three visual directions for the Sales list view (the first MVP module). All three implement the same defining experience; they differ only in visual density and hierarchy treatment.

Direction A — Tally-Dense Classic

Maximum information density. Spreadsheet-like. Status as inline colored text. No row hover, no stripes. Best for: owner who lives in the data and wants the most rows on screen.

Recommendation: Direction B (Linear-Calm Balanced)

Best alignment with the principles from Steps 3-4: density without crampedness, calm without sterility, modern conventions without enterprise overhead. Direction A is a viable alternative if the owner prefers maximum information density. Direction C is rejected — the metric-tile aggregate strip conflicts with the density-over-whitespace principle and won't fit at 320px.
